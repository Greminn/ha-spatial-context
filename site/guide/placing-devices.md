# Placing Devices

**Place Device** puts a Home Assistant device on the plan at a position and mounting height.

![The device picker open beside the floor plan](/screenshots/screenshot-picker-v2.jpeg)

1. Select **Place Device**. The picker opens beside the plan, or as a sheet on a phone.
2. Search, or filter by floor and area.
3. Select a device to arm it, then click the plan.

A device is placed in one location only: one floor, or outdoors. A device placed elsewhere is greyed out in the picker with its location, and has to be removed there first.

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Stack devices on one spot | Click an existing pin while placing. |
| Choose among stacked devices | Select the spot, then pick from its info card. |
| Set label, icon and mounting height | Select the device and use its info card. |
| Clear every placed device on the floor | **⋮** in the picker. Asks first. |

Mounting height is included in the [map data](/ai/map-data).

Devices outside every building are placed on the [Property tab](/guide/property-tab#outdoor-devices).
